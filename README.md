### About me

- Running **SmartMenu** since 2023: web design, digital restaurant menus, business and home automation, 3D printing
- 12 client websites shipped, most of them with an admin panel, a database and GDPR-ready legal pages
- I like projects that run on free tiers, work offline or local-first, and are simple for the client to maintain
- Currently learning **Rust**
- Open to developer roles
